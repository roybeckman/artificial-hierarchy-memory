# AHM — History and provenance

AHM (Artificial Hierarchy Memory) was created and developed by **Roy Beckman** in 2026.

The original June 2026 public revision is preserved under [`history/`](./history/) and remains part of the public provenance of the project. AHM 1.0 simplifies and generalizes those early ideas around the map principle while preserving continuity, local keys, explicit relations, state, and next actions.
